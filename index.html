01structure

A fragmented bazaar.

No one dominates. Hundreds of galleries cluster in a few bazaars, a handful of domestic mills compete with Turkish and Chinese imports, and big platforms capture online search. Laramob is in every bazaar and every physical channel, but absent from the platforms where search now starts.

market structure, from the site audit and competitor researchsquares mark Laramob
who sells
Hundreds of galleries grouped in a few bazaars, a handful of national mills, importers of Turkish and Chinese cloth, and the large platforms that capture online search.No player dominates. Laramob is one of the widest networks, with 6 stores and 3,785 products.

Furniture makers (33 are named as clients on laramob.com), upholsterers who work from sample books, and households re-covering a sofa.Trade buyers still close deals in showrooms; households increasingly start online.

Instagram as the shop window, WhatsApp, Telegram or Rubika for the order, comparison sites for the price.Laramob holds the first two and is missing from the third.
what Laramob covers
All three Tehran hubs, plus Karaj and Isfahan, and every physical channel.Comparison sites and brand websites with visible prices are the two channels where it is absent or weak.
02bazaars

Four places where the trade happens.

Three hubs in Tehran and one production base. Laramob's six stores sit in all three Tehran hubs, with branches in Karaj and Isfahan.

tehran · یافت آباد

Yaftabad

Tehran's main furniture and fabric bazaar. Local directories (Behtarino, Royal Mobl) list dozens of shops. Laramob's central store is on Shandiz Street, the same street as Pershian Outlet.

On the search پارچه مبلی یافت آباد, directories take the top places (Aghaye Mobl, Savadecor, Behtarino); Laramob appears only inside them.

tehran · دلاوران

Delavaran

More than 80 fabric, leather and curtain galleries, per the bazaar's own Instagram account @delavaran.textile (1,712 followers). Laramob has a branch here.

The wood and furniture district of south Tehran. Laramob and Arshin both run showrooms here; it is Arshin's central showroom.

03supply

Iranian mills, Turkish style, Chinese volume.

Three sources feed the market. Turkish origin is the strongest selling argument online, Chinese mills are starting to sell direct in Persian, and imports run at about 600 million dollars a year.

iran

National mills

Producers such as Baya (founded 1376, or 1997), Tat Nasaj Iranian, Adak Premium, Royal Faipco and Nasajit, from the Namayeshgahha list of top manufacturers. Adak sells direct and shows its OEKO-TEX Standard 100 certificate.

türkiye

The quality benchmark

The selling argument used most online. پارچه مبلی ترک (Turkish upholstery fabric) is a search of its own, where Gallery Venus presents itself as “official representative”. Türkiye exported $3.3B of home textiles in 2024.

china

Volume, now direct

Direct importers (SRM in Yaftabad) and Chinese mills that target Iran in Persian, such as Haining Leading Textile (velvet, chenille, leather and linen since 2011), 4th on تولید کننده پارچه مبلی (manufacturer).

04conditions

Cash, cards and the rial.

Three conditions shape how fabric is sold: a currency that moves every day, payment that mostly happens off the website, and prices that only a few sellers dare to publish.

unstable rial

Cash until further notice

Laramob's site states that all sales are cash-only until further notice because of currency swings, announced as an image in its home carousel. Gallery Venus says Turkish fabric prices change “day by day” with the dollar. An unpaid pro-forma invoice on laramob.com is re-priced at the day's stock and prices.

little online payment

Paid off the site

Card-to-card transfers, orders over WhatsApp, Telegram or Rubika. A few offer instalments: Adak through SnappPay in 4 payments, Royal Mobl by cheques. Laramob issues a pro-forma invoice; the buyer transfers the money outside the site (card-to-card or SHEBA) and then declares it in a form.

published prices

A few show, most hide

Ghavareh: 569,000 to 1,250,000 toman per metre. Adak: 320,000 to 1,000,000. Asa: about 1,200,000. Laramob's structured data declares 15.5 to 58.3 million rials, or 1.55 to 5.83 million toman (unit not stated, probably per metre), clearly above what rivals show online.
